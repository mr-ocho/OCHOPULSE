Overview
This is not a place. This is not a city. This is not a home.

This is the run. The space between one step and the next. The blur of the world when you move too fast to see it. The silence after you have left everyone behind.

This is Kael's world.

He has been running his whole life. He does not know how to stop. He does not know how to stay. He does not know how to be anything but a blur.

He can run 10 times the speed of light. But he cannot outrun what he is.

This is where his story begins.

The Location
Kael does not have a location.

He has the run. The endless, breathless, terrified run.

He runs through cities. He runs through forests. He runs through deserts. He runs through time. He runs through everything.

He does not stop. He does not rest. He does not sleep. Not really. He just runs.

He has run from the Family. He has run from Caine. He has run from himself. He has run until he cannot run anymore.

And then he runs again.

The Feeling
Kael is afraid.

Not of death. Not of pain. Not of losing.

He is afraid of stopping.

Because when he stops, he remembers. He remembers the Family. He remembers Caine. He remembers everything he did. Everything he was. Everything he destroyed.

He does not want to remember.

So he runs.

The Emotion
Kael carries the weight of everything he has done.

He does not show it. He does not talk about it. He does not let anyone in. But it is there. Under the speed. Under the fear. Under the running.

He remembers every face. Every name. Every person he hurt. Every person he failed.

He carries them with him. He always will.

He does not know how to put them down.

The Mystery
Kael does not talk about his past.

He does not talk about the Family. He does not talk about Caine. He does not talk about the things he did before he ran.

But there is something there. Something buried. Something he cannot reach.

When he dreams, he sees flashes. A face. A voice. A hand reaching for him.

He does not know what it means. He does not know who it is. But it is there. Waiting. Watching.

And when he finds out, it will change everything.

The Science
Kael is not a god. He is not a metahuman. He is not a mutant. He is something else.

Superluminal Velocity:

In physics, nothing with mass can reach the speed of light. The energy required is infinite. But Kael can exceed it. His body does not obey the normal rules. His mass increases. His time dilates. But he does not break. He runs.

Time Dilation:

According to Einstein's theory of special relativity, the faster you move, the slower time passes for you. Kael experiences time differently. When he runs at 10x light speed, a second for him is years for everyone else. He can cross the Earth in a blink. He can dodge anything. He can outrun anything.

The Cost:

Kael's speed is killing him. Every time he runs, his body breaks. His bones fracture. His muscles tear. His skin burns. He can run 10x light speed. But it is destroying him.

He does not know how much time he has left. He does not want to know.
