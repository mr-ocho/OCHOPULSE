The Realm Between Realms
Overview
This is not Hallow Bay. This is not Earth. This is not any place you would find on a map.

This is Draven's world. The edges. The places between places. The realms where gods live and die. The void where stories end.

Draven was not born in Hallow Bay. He was not born anywhere. He was made. Forged. Created by the Void King to end the gods.

He has walked through Asgard. He has stood in Olympus. He has watched realms burn. He has killed gods whose names have been forgotten. He has ended stories that were never supposed to end.

Hallow Bay is just a stop. A moment. A breath between kills.

This is where he comes from.

The Location
Draven's world is not a place. It is every place. Every realm. Every pantheon. Every story that ends.

He walks the branches of the World Tree. He stands at the gates of Valhalla. He watches the gods of Olympus argue. He passes through the realms of the forgotten. He is everywhere and nowhere.

The realms are not separate. They are connected. Not by space. Not by time. By story. Every realm is a story. Every god is a character. Every ending is Draven's work.

He does not choose where he goes. He is drawn. Pulled. Pointed. He is a weapon. Weapons do not choose their targets.

He has been to Asgard. He has walked its golden halls. He has seen Thor's arrogance. He has seen Odin's weariness. He has seen Loki's pain. He has seen them all. He has judged them all.

He has been to Olympus. He has stood before Zeus's throne. He has seen the king of the gods tremble. He has seen the fear in Athena's eyes. He has seen the madness in Ares's heart.

He has been to the realms of the forgotten. The gods no one worships anymore. The stories no one tells. The endings no one remembers.

He has ended them all.

The Feeling
Draven does not feel. That is what he tells himself.

He does not feel the cold of the void. He does not feel the heat of Olympus. He does not feel the weight of his armor. He does not feel the blood on his hands.

He is a weapon. Weapons do not feel.

But he does feel. He feels something.

He feels tired. He feels empty. He feels the weight of every god he has killed. Every realm he has destroyed. Every story he has ended.

He carries them. Not on his back. In his chest. In the place where a heart should be.

He does not know why. He does not know how. But he carries them.

And he is tired.

The Emotion
Draven is afraid.

Not of death. Not of pain. Not of losing.

He is afraid of what he is. He is afraid that he cannot stop. He is afraid that one day, he will kill something that did not deserve to die. He is afraid that he already has.

He is afraid of the quiet. The silence between kills. The moments when he has nothing to do but think. The moments when he remembers.

He does not want to remember. He does not want to think. He does not want to feel.

But he does.

The Mystery
Draven does not remember his childhood.

He does not remember his parents. He does not remember his home. He does not remember being anything but a weapon.

But there is something there. Something buried. Something he cannot reach.

When he dreams, he sees flashes. A face. A voice. A hand reaching for him.

He does not know what it means. He does not know who it is. But it is there. Waiting. Watching.

And when he finds out, it will change everything.

The Science
Draven is not a god. He is not a cosmic being. He is not a mutant. He is a weapon. But his power is not magic. It is not divine. It is something else.

Narrative Immunity:

Draven exists outside every story. Every narrative. Every destiny. He is not part of any plot. He is not bound by any fate. He is not protected by any plot armor.

This means he cannot be affected by anything within a story. A god cannot kill him because a god is part of a story. Draven is not. He is outside it.

Destiny Nullification:

Thor cannot die in battle because destiny says so. Draven is not part of that destiny. He can kill Thor because Thor's fate does not apply to him.

Superman is protected by narrative. Draven is outside narrative. He ends stories.

Zeus relies on authority and fear. Draven is immune to both.

Kratos is protected by a curse. Draven ends curses.

The God-Killer Principle:

Draven is the End of Gods. He is not stronger than gods. He is not faster than gods. He is outside the rules that protect them. He can kill them because their rules do not apply to him.

He does not fight gods. He ends them.
