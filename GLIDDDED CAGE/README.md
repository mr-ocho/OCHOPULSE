### Setting

Hallow Bay is a coastal city on the eastern seaboard. It was once a thriving trade hub. Now it is a monument to inequality.

The wealthy live in the Heights. A cluster of glass towers and private estates overlooking the bay.

The poor live in the Lowlands. A sprawling slum of crumbling apartments and abandoned factories.

Between them is the Bay itself. A body of water that separates the rich from the poor. A reminder that distance is not measured in miles. It is measured in money.

The city is run by the Council. Five families who control everything. They own the politicians. They own the police. They own the media. They own the heroes, too. Some of them.

But not all of them.

---

### The Era

2016 to 2020. Modern. Connected. Watched.

Smartphones. Social media. 4G. Streaming. Drones. Cameras everywhere.

Viral videos. 24-hour news. Online outrage. Memes.

Heroes cannot hide. They are exposed. They are feared. They are hunted.

---

### The Tone

Grounded. Real. Dark. But hopeful.

The heroes are not perfect. They are broken. They are trying.

The villains are not evil. They are broken. They are lost.

The city is corrupt. But it can be saved.

---

### The Watcher

Jaxo does not appear in Universe 1. He is not in the story. Not physically.

But he is seen. Very few times. In the background. In the silence. In the moments between moments.

When the battle ends. When the villains fall. When the heroes stand victorious.

For a moment, everything stops.

The cracks freeze. The wind dies. The air goes cold.

And in the silence, something watches.

Not a man. Not a god. Not anything.

Just a presence. A feeling. A weight.

It is there for a second. Then it is gone.

Some feel it. Some do not. Some cannot.

They stand there. Silent. The battle is over. But something else has begun.

Something none of them understand.
