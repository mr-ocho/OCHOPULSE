Overview
This is not a place you will find on any map. This is not a place the rich talk about. This is not a place the world remembers.

This is the Lowlands. The forgotten part of a city that has no name. The part where people live and die and no one notices.

This is Mira's world. The people. The pain. The hope. The place where she fights.

She was born here. She grew up here. She protects here. She will die here if she has to.

This is where her story begins.

The Location
The Lowlands is not a place. It is a wound.

It is the part of the city that was abandoned. The factories closed. The jobs left. The people stayed. They had nowhere else to go.

The buildings are crumbling. The streets are cracked. The air tastes like rust and regret. Children play in the rubble. Mothers work three jobs. Fathers die young.

The Lowlands is where Vonac was born. Where Mira was born. Where Kael found himself. Where the forgotten live.

It is not on the postcards. It is not in the brochures. It is not in the speeches the rich give about progress and prosperity.

It is the truth. The truth no one wants to see.

The Feeling
Mira belongs here.

She was born in the Lowlands. She grew up with rust in the air and hope in her heart. She knows what it is to be hungry. To be cold. To be forgotten.

But she did not let it break her. She let it make her.

She walks through the Lowlands like she belongs. Because she does. She talks to the people. She knows their names. She knows their stories. She knows their pain.

She is not a hero to them. She is Mira. The girl who grew up on their street. The girl who can bend reality. The girl who stayed.

She protects them. Not because she has to. Because she wants to.

The Emotion
Mira carries the weight of everyone she could not save.

She does not show it. She does not talk about it. She does not let anyone in. But it is there. Under the surface. Under the smile. Under the heart.

She is the heart. She is the hope. But she is also broken.

She remembers every face. Every name. Every person she could not save. She carries them with her. She always will.

She does not know how to put them down.

The Mystery
Mira has a secret.

She has never told anyone. Not her mother. Not her friends. Not the team. She has never told anyone what she saw the night her father died.

She was there. She saw it. She saw who did it.

She has never told anyone.

She does not know what to do with the truth. She does not know who to trust. She does not know if she can even trust herself.

But it is there. Waiting. Watching.

And when it comes out, it will change everything.

The Science
Mira is not a mutant. She is not a metahuman. She is not a god. She is something else.

Reality Warping:

Mira can bend reality. She can alter the fabric of existence. She can change what is. She can create. She can destroy. She can reshape.

This is not magic. It is not divine. It is something else. Something science cannot fully explain. Something that exists at the edge of what is possible.

Chaos Magic:

Mira's power is not ordered. It is not controlled. It is chaos. Raw. Primordial. It responds to her will. But it also responds to her emotions. When she is calm, it is controlled. When she is angry, it is devastating.

Divine Strength:

Mira is as strong as Superman. She is as fast as the Flash. She can fly. She is invulnerable to most weapons. She is not a god. But she is something close.
