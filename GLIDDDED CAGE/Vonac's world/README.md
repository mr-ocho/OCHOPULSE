
Overview
This is Vonac's world. Hallow Bay. The city. The Heights. The Lowlands. The Council. The corruption. The technology. The cold, calculated world of the rich and powerful.

Vonac was born in the Lowlands, but he operates in the Heights. He knows their secrets. He knows their weaknesses. He hunts them from the shadows.

This is where his story begins.

This is where they all meet.

The Location
Hallow Bay is not a place you will find on any map. It is a coastal city on the eastern seaboard. It was once a thriving trade hub. Now it is a monument to inequality.

The wealthy live in the Heights. A cluster of glass towers and private estates overlooking the bay.

The poor live in the Lowlands. A sprawling slum of crumbling apartments and abandoned factories.

Between them is the Bay itself. A body of water that separates the rich from the poor. A reminder that distance is not measured in miles. It is measured in money.

The city is run by the Council. Five families who control everything. They own the politicians. They own the police. They own the media. They own the heroes, too. Some of them.

But not all of them.

The Feeling
Vonac does not belong here.

He was born in the Lowlands. He grew up with rust in the air and grease under his nails. He knows what it is to be hungry. To be cold. To be forgotten.

Now he walks among the rich. He wears their clothes. He speaks their language. He moves through their world like he belongs. But he does not. He never will.

He hates them. He hates their ease. He hates their confidence. He hates the way they look at him like he is a curiosity. A pet. A project.

But he does not hate all of them.

He hated Elias once. No. He loved Elias once. Like a brother. Like a part of himself.

Now Elias is gone. And Vonac is alone.

He walks through the Heights. He watches. He waits. He plans.

He is the mind. He is always ten steps ahead.

But he is also broken. He is also alone. He is also searching for something he lost a long time ago.

The Emotion
Vonac is not a hero. He is a problem.

But he is also a man. A man who lost his parents. A man who lost his best friend. A man who lost himself.

He does not show it. He does not talk about it. He does not let anyone in. But it is there. Under the surface. Under the armor. Under the mind.

He is angry. He is hurt. He is alone.

He does not know how to be anything else.

The Mystery
There is something Vonac is not telling us.

Something about Elias. Something about the night Nac Technologies burned. Something about the seven years he spent alone.

He does not talk about it. He does not think about it. He does not let himself remember.

But it is there. Waiting. Watching.

And when Elias comes back, it will come back with him.

The Science
Vonac has no superpowers. He is human. His power is his mind.

Neural Efficiency:

He trained his brain to use more of its capacity. Not because he was enhanced. Because he taught himself how.

Pattern Recognition:

He sees patterns where others see noise. He can look at a crowd and see the exits. He can look at a building and see the weakness. He can look at a person and see the lie.

Predictive Modeling:

He does not predict the future. He models it. He takes in data. He runs simulations in his head. He considers every variable. He calculates every probability.

Technological Aptitude:

He built everything he uses. His suit. His gadgets. His AI. He understands it because he created it.
