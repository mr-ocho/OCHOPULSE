Overview
This is not a place. This is not a city. This is not a time.

This is the space between seconds. The breath between heartbeats. The silence after a word is spoken but before it is heard.

This is Maximus's world.

He does not live in a city. He does not live in a time. He lives in the cracks. The moments everyone else forgets. The places where time is thin enough to step through.

He has seen every future. He has walked every past. He has stood in every present. He is everywhere. He is nowhere. He is the witness.

This is where his story begins.

The Location
Maximus does not have a location.

He exists in the gaps. The spaces between moments. The places where time folds in on itself. The seconds that stretch into eternities. The eternities that collapse into seconds.

When he stands still, he is not standing. He is waiting. When he walks, he is not walking. He is stepping through time.

He can be in a city street in 2016 and a battlefield in 1347 in the same breath. He can watch a child being born and an old man dying in the same moment. He can see the beginning and the end at once.

He is not in Hallow Bay. He is not in any city. He is not in any time.

He is in the space between.

The Feeling
Maximus does not feel time. He is time. Or he was. Or he will be. It is difficult to explain.

He does not age. He does not sleep. He does not eat. He does not need to. He exists outside the normal flow of things.

But he does feel. He feels tired. He feels heavy. He feels the weight of every future he has seen. Every past he has walked. Every present he has stood in.

He has seen too much. He has seen everyone he loves die in every possible way. He has seen every failure. Every mistake. Every loss.

He carries them all. Every version. Every possibility. Every death.

He does not know how to put them down.

The Emotion
Maximus is afraid.

Not of death. Not of pain. Not of losing.

He is afraid of the one future he cannot see.

There is a moment. A single moment. A place where time itself goes dark. He has tried to see it. He has tried to reach it. He has tried to understand it.

He cannot.

It is blank. Empty. Silent. Like a hole in the fabric of everything.

He does not know what it means. He does not know what happens. But he knows it is coming.

And he is afraid.

The Mystery
Maximus does not remember his life before the accident.

He remembers being a student. He remembers studying temporal physics. He remembers the lab. He remembers the explosion.

But he does not remember who he was. Not really. Not fully.

He does not remember his family. He does not remember his friends. He does not remember his dreams. He does not remember the person he was before time rushed through him.

He has seen every future. But he cannot see his own past.

There is a hole in his memory. A gap. A place where something should be but is not.

He does not know what happened. He does not know what he lost.

But he knows something is missing.

And he is afraid to find out what.

The Science
Maximus is not a magician. He is not a sorcerer. He is not a god. He is something else.

Temporal Perception:

The human brain perceives time linearly. Past. Present. Future. One moment after another. Maximus does not. His brain perceives time as a landscape. A block. Every moment exists simultaneously. Past, present, and future are locations. He can see them all.

Block Universe Theory:

In physics, the Block Universe Theory suggests that time is not a river. It is a landscape. Past, present, and future all exist simultaneously. Every moment is a location. Maximus can see the entire landscape. He can move through it. Not time travel. Time navigation.

Temporal Manipulation:

Maximus can slow time. Stop time. Rewind time. Fast-forward time. He can age objects to dust or restore them to new. He can trap someone in a moment. He can loop a second over and over.

He does not create time. He does not destroy time. He manipulates it.
