DRAVEN
THE END OF GODS
WHO HE IS
Draven is not a hero. He is not a villain. He is not a god.

He is a weapon.

He was made to end gods. He was made to destroy realms. He was made to close stories. He is the thing that gods fear. He is the thing that gods cannot stop.

He is the End of Gods.

He does not want to be this. He does not want to kill. He does not want to destroy. But it is what he was made for. It is what he is.

He is the weapon. The inevitable. The end.

HOW HE WAS FORMED
Draven does not remember his childhood. He does not remember his parents. He does not remember his home.

He remembers waking up. He remembers being told what he was. He remembers being pointed at his first god.

He was created by the Void King. Something older than the gods. Something that watched the gods rise. Something that watched them become arrogant. Cruel. Dangerous.

The Void King knew they had to be stopped. But he could not stop them. He was a god of nothing. He could not create. He could not destroy. He could only exist.

So he made something that could.

He made a son.

He poured everything into him. His power. His will. His purpose. He gave him the ability to end gods. To end anything.

But he also gave him something else. Something he did not mean to give.

He gave him a heart.

Then the Void King changed. He became arrogant. He believed he knew best. He believed he was the only one who could save the multiverse. He hid Draven. Not to protect him. To protect himself.

Draven does not know this. He thinks he is a weapon. He thinks he was made. He thinks he is nothing but a tool.

But he is not. He is a son. And the Void King is still out there.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND HIS POWER
Draven is not a god. He is not a cosmic being. He is not a mutant. He is a weapon. But his power is not magic. It is not divine. It is something else.

Narrative Immunity:

Draven exists outside every story. Every narrative. Every destiny. He is not part of any plot. He is not bound by any fate. He is not protected by any plot armor.

This means he cannot be affected by anything within a story. A god cannot kill him because a god is part of a story. Draven is not. He is outside it.

Destiny Nullification:

Thor cannot die in battle because destiny says so. Draven is not part of that destiny. He can kill Thor because Thor's fate does not apply to him.

Superman is protected by narrative. Draven is outside narrative. He ends stories.

Zeus relies on authority and fear. Draven is immune to both.

Kratos is protected by a curse. Draven ends curses.

The God-Killer Principle:

Draven is the End of Gods. He is not stronger than gods. He is not faster than gods. He is outside the rules that protect them. He can kill them because their rules do not apply to him.

He does not fight gods. He ends them.
