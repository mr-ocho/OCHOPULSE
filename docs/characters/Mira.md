MIRA
THE HEART
WHO SHE IS
Mira is not a soldier. She is not a weapon. She is not a god.

She is the heart.

She is the one who holds the team together. The one who reminds everyone why they fight. The one who believes in people even when they do not believe in themselves.

She is warm. She is hopeful. She is fierce. She is loyal. She is gentle. She is unbreakable.

She is the heart.

HOW SHE WAS FORMED
Mira was born in Hallow Bay. The lower districts. The parts of the city the rich pretend do not exist.

Her mother was a nurse. Her father was a factory worker. They were poor. They were happy. They were good people.

When Mira was twelve, her powers manifested. She did not understand them. She did not control them. She accidentally hurt someone. A boy at school who was bullying her. She did not mean to. She just wanted him to stop. He flew across the room and hit the wall. He survived. But he never looked at her the same way again.

Her parents tried to help her. They took her to doctors. Specialists. No one could explain what she was.

When she was sixteen, her father was killed in a factory accident. The same factory that had killed Vonac's father. The same corruption. The same silence.

Mira could have used her power to take revenge. She wanted to. But her mother stopped her. "That is not who you are," she said. "You are not vengeance. You are hope."

Mira never forgot those words.

She trained. She learned to control her power. She learned to fight. She became a hero. Not because she wanted to be. Because she had to.

She protects Hallow Bay. Not the rich. Not the powerful. The people. The ones who cannot protect themselves.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND HER POWER
Mira is not a mutant. She is not a metahuman. She is not a god. She is something else.

Reality Warping:

Mira can bend reality. She can alter the fabric of existence. She can change what is. She can create. She can destroy. She can reshape.

This is not magic. It is not divine. It is something else. Something science cannot fully explain. Something that exists at the edge of what is possible.

Chaos Magic:

Mira's power is not ordered. It is not controlled. It is chaos. Raw. Primordial. It responds to her will. But it also responds to her emotions. When she is calm, it is controlled. When she is angry, it is devastating.

Divine Strength:

Mira is as strong as Superman. She is as fast as the Flash. She can fly. She is invulnerable to most weapons. She is not a god. But she is something close.

The Limits:

Limit	Description
Energy Cost	Using her full power drains her life force.
Emotional Control	Her power is tied to her emotions. If she loses control, she loses herself.
Cannot Save Everyone	She cannot save everyone. No matter how hard she tries.
Mortality	She is not invincible. She can be hurt. She can be killed.
The Cost:

Mira feels every death she could not prevent. Every life she could not save. Every person she failed. She carries the weight of every failure. It does not make her weaker. It makes her more determined.

HER PERSONALITY
Mira is warm and hopeful. She is the heart of the team. The one who reminds everyone why they fight. The one who believes in people even when they do not believe in themselves.

She is fierce and loyal. She will fight for her friends. She will die for them. But she will also cry for them. She will grieve. She will feel.

She is gentle but unbreakable. She has been through pain. She has lost people. But she has not let it harden her. She has let it make her kinder.

She is stubborn. She does not give up. She does not stop. She does not let go. Even when she should. Even when it hurts. Even when it is impossible.

Her Voice:

Warm. Soft. But when she needs to be, sharp as a blade.

HER FLAW
Mira cares too much.

She cannot save everyone. She knows this. But she tries anyway. Every time she fails, it breaks a piece of her. She carries the weight of every life she could not protect.

She is too trusting. She sees the good in people even when there is none. She gives second chances. Third chances. Fourth. Sometimes it works. Sometimes it gets people killed.

She is afraid of her own power. She knows what she can do. She knows what she could become if she lost control. She lives in fear of the day she stops holding back.
