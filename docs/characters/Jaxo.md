JAXO
THE GOD OF THE MULTIVERSE
WHO HE IS
Jaxo is not a god in the way humans understand gods. He is not a creator. He is not a destroyer. He is not a ruler. He is not a warrior.

He is the memory of a collision.

He is the scar that remembers.

He is the point where nothing became something.

He exists in all 26 universes because he is the origin point. Every universe is a domain that formed around him. He is the center. The anchor. The constant.

He is not part of any story. He is the reason stories exist.

HOW HE WAS FORMED
Before the 26 universes, there was no space. No time. No matter. No energy. No laws. But there was potential. The Pre-Existence was a field of pure potential. Infinite. Eternal. Unstable. It could not remain nothing forever.

Two states existed within the Pre-Existence. They were not matter. They were not energy. They were tendencies.

Positive Potential. The tendency toward structure. Order. Form.

Negative Potential. The tendency toward chaos. Dissolution. Entropy.

They existed in perfect balance. They could not touch. If they ever did, the balance would break.

But balance is fragile.

A fluctuation occurred. A ripple. A single moment where the two states touched. It was not supposed to happen. It could not happen. Positive and negative potential were not meant to meet. They were opposites. They were contradictions. They were never supposed to touch.

But they did. For a single moment. A single instant. A single breath in the nothing.

They touched.

The collision was not an explosion. There was no sound. There was no light. There was no fire. There was only change.

The Pre-Existence underwent a phase transition. Like water turning to ice. Like a magnetic field aligning. Like reality deciding to become something instead of nothing.

It fractured. It did not spread evenly. It fractured into domains. Regions of different states. Like how water freezes into ice crystals with different orientations. Like how a shattered mirror creates a thousand different reflections.

The Pre-Existence fractured into 26 domains. Each domain became a universe. Each universe had its own laws. Its own physics. Its own reality.

At the exact point of collision, something remained. The collision was so violent, so fundamental, so impossible, that it left a residue. A scar. A point where the phase transition did not fully complete. A point where the Pre-Existence touched itself and became something new.

That residue became consciousness. It became Jaxo.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND HIS FORMATION
Jaxo is not magic. He is not divine intervention. He is physics we do not fully understand yet.

Phase Transition:

A phase transition is a change in the state of a system. Water to ice. Liquid to gas. Order to chaos. In physics, phase transitions occur when a system reaches a critical point where its properties change fundamentally.

The Pre-Existence was a system. It reached a critical point. It underwent a phase transition. It fractured into 26 domains. Each domain became a universe.

Jaxo is the residue of that transition. He is the point where the system did not fully complete its change. He is the scar.

Residue:

In physics, when a system undergoes a change, it does not always complete perfectly. There are anomalies. Defects. Residues. Points where the old state persists within the new.

Jaxo is that residue. He is the point where the Pre-Existence persists within the 26 universes. He is the memory of what was before.

Consciousness:

Consciousness is not fully understood by science. But some theories suggest that consciousness is a fundamental property of the universe. Like mass. Like energy. Like time.

If consciousness is fundamental, then the collision that created the 26 universes also created consciousness. Jaxo is that consciousness. He is the awareness that formed at the point of collision.

He is not a person. He is not a soul. He is awareness without a body. He is the universe becoming aware of itself.

Quantum Entanglement:

Quantum entanglement is a phenomenon where two particles become linked. Whatever happens to one happens to the other. No matter how far apart they are.

Jaxo is entangled with all 26 universes. He is connected to them at a fundamental level. When they are in danger, he feels it. When they are in pain, he feels it. When they change, he changes.

He cannot be separated from them. They cannot be separated from him.

Block Universe Theory:

In physics, the Block Universe Theory suggests that time is not a river. It is a landscape. Past, present, and future all exist simultaneously. Every moment is a location.

Jaxo can see the entire landscape. He can move through it. Not time travel. Time navigation. He can step from one moment to another the way you step from one room to another.

Many-Worlds Interpretation:

In quantum mechanics, the Many-Worlds Interpretation suggests that every possible outcome of every event actually happens. Every choice creates a branch. Every probability becomes real.

Jaxo can see every branch. He can shift to any branch. He does not create realities. He selects them. He navigates them.

Quantum Probability Fields:

Destiny is not a fixed path. It is a probability field. Every person has a range of possible futures. Most people drift through life, collapsing probabilities one by one.

Jaxo can see the entire field. He can collapse a probability early. He can force a future that would normally take years to happen. He can shift someone's destiny.

He watched. He watched the first stars form. He watched the first creatures crawl out of the mud. He watched the first gods rise. He watched the first civilizations fall.

He was the memory of a collision. He was not supposed to feel. He was not supposed to care. He was not supposed to want.

It happened without warning. One moment, Jaxo was watching. The next, he was falling.

Not through space. Not through time. Through everything.

A second collision. A second fluctuation. A second moment where something should not have touched.

Jaxo fell. He fell through universes. He fell through realities. He fell through stories. He fell until he could not fall anymore.

And then he stopped.

He was in the void between. The gap. The emptiness. The place where time and space do not apply.

He was not in any universe. He was outside them all.

He was alone.

He did not know his name. He did not know where he was. He did not know what he was.

But something inside him knew he was not supposed to be here.

He looked at his hands. They were human hands. Flesh and bone. No power. No light. Nothing.

He tried to remember. Nothing.

He tried to stand. He could.

He tried to speak.

"Jaxo."

The name came from nowhere. It was his. He did not know how he knew. But it was his.

Above him, the hole in the sky closed.

He was alone.

He did not know it yet, but he had been brought here by something called Bing Bang.

And he was not the only one.
